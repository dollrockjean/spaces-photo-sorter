Spaces Photo Sorter: privacy

Spaces Photo Sorter is an internal tool for Spaces Designer Homes. It helps staff sort job photos in Google Drive into folders and rename them.

What the tool can see

When you sign in with Google, you let the tool view and move files in Google Drive on your behalf. It uses this only to show thumbnails of the photos in the folder you open, create folders, and move and rename photos that you choose.

What is stored

Nothing is sent to or stored on any server run by us. The tool runs in your web browser and talks directly to Google Drive. A short history of your last moves is kept in your own browser so you can undo them. It never leaves your computer.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or send your Google data to anyone. The tool does not use your Google data for advertising.

Signing out

You can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
